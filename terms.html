Terms of Use

Draft — adult legal review and completion required before public launch.

HelpingHeart Marketplace provides a listing and communication service. It does not verify every user, item, description, price or claim, and it does not process payments or guarantee transactions.

User responsibilities

  • Provide accurate, lawful listings and only offer items you are authorised to sell.
  • Do not list illegal, stolen, counterfeit, recalled, hazardous, age-restricted or otherwise prohibited items.
  • Do not impersonate, threaten, harass, deceive or misuse another member's information.
  • Do not publish passwords, OTPs, financial credentials, private contact details or home addresses.
  • Use report and block tools for safety concerns. Reports may be reviewed by authorised moderators.

Transactions

Buyers and sellers independently arrange any transaction. HelpingHeart does not hold funds, offer escrow, arrange shipping, guarantee payment or resolve disputes. Minors must not independently arrange in-person transactions; an adult must be involved and choose safe public arrangements.

Moderation

Listings may be paused or removed when they appear to violate these rules or create safety risks. Accounts or content may be restricted following review. A report is not proof of wrongdoing.

Contact and legal details

The service operator must add a real support contact, legal entity/operator details, governing law, jurisdiction, complaint handling and effective date after adult legal review. Do not publish until these are completed.

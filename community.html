Community Guidelines

Draft — adult operator review required before public launch.

  • Be honest about ownership, condition, price, images and item defects.
  • No illegal, stolen, counterfeit, recalled, hazardous, age-restricted or prohibited items.
  • No scams, fake payment confirmations, impersonation, harassment, threats or discriminatory abuse.
  • Never request or share passwords, OTPs, bank details, card details or identity documents in chat.
  • Do not publish home addresses, private phone numbers, school details or other sensitive personal information.
  • Do not pressure anyone to meet privately. In-person arrangements must involve adults and safe public locations.

Report and block

Use Report listing or Report member to flag genuine concerns. Blocking prevents new messages between the two accounts. Reports enter a moderation queue for authorised administrators. Do not use reporting to harass or retaliate against another member.

Moderation

Authorised administrators may review reports and pause or remove listings. Admin access must be granted securely using Firebase custom claims, never by editing a public profile.
